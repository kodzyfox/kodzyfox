## Featured Projects

### [AURA](https://github.com/kodzyfox/AURA) — Native macOS Ambient Music Companion & Ambilight Visualizer
Pure native macOS app (SwiftUI, Metal, Accelerate vDSP) with zero dependencies. Real-time audio frequency analysis, screen-edge Ambilight sync with Spotify & Apple Music, and dynamic wallpapers.

### [2pchat](https://github.com/Nulliik/2pchat) — Decentralized Peer-to-Peer Messenger
Encrypted P2P messaging with Go core, Android client (Kotlin / Jetpack Compose), Yggdrasil mesh routing, and authenticated sessions. *(Releases & builds available on [2pchat-releases](https://github.com/kodzyfox/2pchat-releases))*.

### [drosophila-telegram-stream](https://github.com/kodzyfox/drosophila-telegram-stream) — 3D FlyBrain Connectome & Telegram Stream
Interactive 3D WebGL simulation combining the *Drosophila melanogaster* connectome (Nature / FlyWire) with a dynamic Telegram feed viewer in Three.js and FastAPI.
